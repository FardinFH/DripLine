FAQs

Most common questions are answered here. If you still need help, contact support.

Common Questions

How do I choose the right size?
Use the size chart on the product page. If you prefer relaxed fit, choose one size up. For regular fit, follow your chest measurement.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery time varies by location and courier workload. You’ll receive tracking once the order is dispatched.
Can I cancel my order?
Yes, if the order hasn’t been dispatched. After dispatch, you can follow the return policy if eligible.
How do returns work?
Return eligibility depends on condition and return window. Keep tags/packaging intact and submit a return request with order details.
What if I receive a wrong or damaged item?
Contact support quickly with unboxing photos/video. After verification, we’ll arrange a replacement or return based on availability.
Do you offer exchanges?
Yes, exchanges depend on stock availability. If your desired size/color is unavailable, we’ll suggest alternatives.
Tip: Save your invoice and keep packaging until the order is fully confirmed.
