Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

  • Account info: name, phone, email if provided
  • Shipping info: address and delivery notes
  • Order history and customer support messages
  • Device/usage info for security and analytics

How We Use Information

  • To process orders, deliver products, and provide customer support
  • To communicate order updates, delivery status, and essential notifications
  • To improve the website experience and protect against fraud

Sharing of Information

We share data only with service providers required to complete your order such as couriers, payment partners, and essential tools. We do not sell customer data.

Security

We use standard security practices to protect customer data. No online system is 100% secure, so please keep your account details private and use a strong password.

Your Choices

  • You can request correction of account information
  • You can opt out of promotional communications where applicable

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.

If you have questions about privacy, contact us through the Contact Us page.
